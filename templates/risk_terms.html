Bitcoin Mining Risks & Terms

Understand the technology, costs and risks before purchasing mining equipment or a mining-related product.

1. Mining is not guaranteed income

Bitcoin mining rewards are variable. A mining operation may earn less than expected or no reward during a period. Any figures, rates or projections displayed by BitForge are estimates only and are not guaranteed returns.

2. Bitcoin price and market volatility

The market price of Bitcoin can rise or fall sharply. The value of mining rewards may decrease, and converting Bitcoin to local currency may result in a loss.

3. Electricity, hardware and operating costs

Mining equipment consumes substantial electricity and produces heat and noise. Electricity prices, cooling, internet connectivity, repairs, equipment wear and replacement parts can reduce or eliminate profitability.

4. Difficulty, competition and reward changes

Bitcoin network mining difficulty changes as miners join or leave. Competition, block-reward halvings, transaction fees and network conditions can affect the amount and value of mining rewards.

5. Downtime, damage and security

Equipment may fail, overheat, be damaged, become obsolete or be affected by power outages, fire, theft, cyberattacks or unauthorized access. No uninterrupted operation is guaranteed.

6. Pool, custody and counterparty risks

Where a mining pool, hosting provider, wallet or exchange is used, its outage, insolvency, withdrawal restrictions, fees, security incident or failure to pay may delay or prevent receipt of funds. BitForge should identify the actual operator and payout source for any represented mining activity.

7. Withdrawals and available funds

A displayed balance is not proof that cash or Bitcoin is available for immediate payment. Withdrawals are subject to identity and fraud checks, applicable fees, processing times, network conditions and sufficient cleared funds in the payout reserve. Do not deposit money you cannot afford to lose.

8. No guaranteed return or principal protection

Mining-related purchases are exposed to loss. There is no guarantee that the purchase price will be recovered, that daily income will be generated, or that any projected rate will be achieved. Past performance does not predict future results.

9. Legal, tax and regulatory obligations

Bitcoin mining, cryptocurrency custody and transfers may be regulated differently depending on your location. You are responsible for understanding applicable laws, tax reporting and any restrictions before participating.

10. Platform terms and changes

BitForge may update product information, fees, security procedures or these terms when necessary. Material changes should be communicated to users. If a product is unavailable or its description is inaccurate, contact support before making a payment.

Important acknowledgement

By continuing, you confirm that you have read these risks and understand that Bitcoin mining is uncertain and can result in partial or total loss. You are not relying on a guaranteed daily return.
